Contracts Manager

Keep track of every contract, subscription and insurance policy in your household – self-hosted, private and shareable.

Get started View on GitHub

Why Contracts Manager

Electricity, internet, mobile, insurance, streaming, the gym – most households have a dozen or more running contracts, each with its own price, payment interval, notice period and paperwork. Contracts Manager brings them together in one place.

Know what you pay

See at a glance what you pay per month and per year, and where the money goes.

Price history

Record price changes over time – the current amount and the history behind it.

Email reminders

Get an email reminder before a notice period ends or a contract should be reviewed.

Share with your household

Share contracts with family members or housemates – read-only or with edit rights.

Your hardware, your data

Built and tested for a Synology NAS with Docker, with your data on an encrypted volume. English and German interface, light and dark mode, works on the phone.

Features

Contracts and costs

  • Partner, category, consumer, payment method, start date, term, contract number, Markdown notes
  • Status as a traffic light: active, notice sent, cancelled, archived, plus the date a cancellation takes effect
  • Predecessor and successor contracts
  • Automatic reference numbers per year (e.g. 20260001)
  • Cost history with weekly, monthly, quarterly or yearly interval; totals per month and year
  • Documents: uploads (PDF, images, Office, up to 20 MB) or Paperless-ngx documents
  • Live search, filters and sorting; totals follow the filter

Master data

  • Contract partners with address, contact and logo
  • Categories as a two-level tree (e.g. Insurance › Car insurance)
  • Consumers: a person, a car, the household
  • Payment methods with icons

Sharing and roles

  • Every object has an owner; share with readers or editors (users or groups)
  • Built-in group to share with everyone
  • User and group management in the web interface, invitations by email, an administrator role

Reminders and email

  • One reminder per contract: date, days in advance, reason, recipients
  • Daily emails from the lead date to the reminder date, then switched off automatically
  • Password reset and invitations by email (any SMTP server)

Integrations

  • Paperless-ngx via its API: documents open inside Contracts Manager, uploads can be transferred with tags and correspondent
  • Single sign-on with the Synology SSO Server (OpenID Connect); local login stays available
  • Backup and import of all data and files as one ZIP file (without passwords)

AI assistant optional, off by default

  • Ask questions about your contracts in a chat
  • Upload a contract or invoice and review the proposed contract
  • Spots probable duplicates in master data
  • See below for details

AI assistant

Optional and off by default.

You stay in control

Upload a contract or invoice; the assistant reads it and proposes the new contract. Nothing is saved until you approve it. The assistant can never delete anything.

Choose your provider

Claude (Anthropic), Mistral AI, OpenAI, OpenAI-compatible providers, or Ollama with local models – nothing leaves the house.

Scans and photos

Local text recognition (Tesseract) or an optional document AI (e.g. Mistral OCR).

Self-hosted & private

Runs at home

Synology, Docker Compose, your own hardware. The app runs as a non-root user.

No CDN, no tracking

All assets are served locally.

Careful with secrets

Credentials are never shown again and never written to a backup. Login lockout after repeated failed attempts.

One-file backup

Back up and import all data and files as a single ZIP file. After every update, each user sees a short “What's new” window once.

Get started

  1. Create a folder with the subfolders db and media (e.g. /volume1/docker/contracts-manager on a Synology).
  2. Download docker-compose.yml into it and replace CHANGE_ME with a database password.
  3. Start it – in the Synology Container Manager (Project › Create) or with docker compose up -d.
  4. Open http://<your-nas>:8010 and create the first administrator in the browser.

No .env file and no SECRET_KEY needed. Email, single sign-on, the AI assistant and Paperless are set up later in the app.

Docker image: brainx9/contracts-manager (Docker Hub), also ghcr.io/brainx9/contracts-manager; linux/amd64 and linux/arm64.
